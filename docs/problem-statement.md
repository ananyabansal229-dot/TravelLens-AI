# TravelLens AI – Problem Statement

## Background

Tourism connects people with different cultures, histories,
architectures, and geographical locations.

However, independent travellers often face difficulties
identifying landmarks and accessing reliable information
without professional tourist guidance.

## Problem

1. Lack of easily accessible landmark information.
2. Difficulty identifying unfamiliar monuments.
3. Language and communication barriers.
4. Difficulty discovering nearby attractions.
5. Limited internet access in some destinations.
6. Difficulty planning efficient travel routes.
7. Lack of verified local stories and cultural knowledge.

## Proposed Solution

TravelLens AI is an AI-powered travel companion that
recognizes landmarks using images and provides relevant
information, nearby discoveries, navigation assistance,
and travel-related services.

## Main Objectives

- Make landmark identification accessible.
- Provide reliable historical and cultural information.
- Improve independent travel experiences.
- Support national and international destinations.
- Encourage verified community knowledge sharing.
- Provide useful offline information.
- Maintain privacy-aware user analytics.

## Target Users

- Domestic tourists.
- International tourists.
- Solo travellers.
- Students and educational travellers.
- History and heritage enthusiasts.
- Local guides and verified contributors.
